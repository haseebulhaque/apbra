# Corporate branding standards

Version: 1.0.0 | Synthetic customer: Contoso Sales Demo

## Project and page naming
Use `SalesPerformance` as the project name. Use short, audience-oriented page names. The primary executive page is `Executive Summary`; a secondary analysis page may be named `Regional Performance`.

Report names should use PascalCase without environment names, dates, employee names, or tenant identifiers. Page labels should describe the decision supported by the page. Avoid technical table names in navigation. When a report serves another business domain, derive its name from that domain rather than reusing the Sales Performance example.

## Theme
Use the fictional Sales Demo theme: white background `#FFFFFF`, text `#172B4D`, and primary accent `#005A9C`. Branding must remain readable and must not imply association with a real customer.

Use the accent colour sparingly for selection and emphasis. Neutral greys should structure the canvas, and semantic success, warning, and error colours must have text or icon reinforcement. Logo placement must not reduce the chart area or imply an external endorsement.

## Executive presentation
Executive pages should lead with a concise title, reporting period, and the few measures required to understand performance. Use consistent number formats and plain business language. Detail pages may offer more analytical context, but they should preserve the same theme, navigation position, filter behaviour, and naming convention.

## Locale and formatting
Apply the tenant locale to dates, separators, and currency labels. Currency measures must state the confirmed currency. Date labels should be unambiguous, and exported artefacts should not silently change locale or timezone assumptions.
