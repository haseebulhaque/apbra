# Accessibility and readability standards

Version: 1.0.0 | Synthetic customer: Contoso Sales Demo

## Accessible presentation
Use meaningful visual titles, readable labels, text alternatives, sufficient contrast, and deliberate keyboard order. Do not convey meaning through colour alone. Record that final accessibility verification requires human and Power BI runtime review.

Text alternatives should explain the purpose and primary comparison without inventing results. Avoid relying on hover-only content. Use familiar language, consistent units, and enough space for labels at expected display sizes.

## Colour and contrast
Text and essential graphical marks must remain distinguishable against the selected theme. Status colours require an additional label, symbol, or pattern. Tenant branding may be adjusted when its original colour combination does not provide adequate readability.

## Keyboard and reading order
The visual tab order should follow the page narrative from title and filters through KPIs and detail. Decorative elements should not interrupt navigation. Final keyboard behaviour, screen-reader output, focus visibility, and high-contrast mode remain runtime checks.
